OpenRA MCP · 自然语言 RTS 战术副官· natural-language RTS co-pilot

2026.06 · 独立研究与开发 · 预印本 + 开源Jun 2026 · solo research · preprint + open source

玩家用一句话说出战术意图，比如「两队钳形包抄，一队佯攻」，LLM 把它转成结构化的 MCP 调用，由 OpenRA 引擎执行。

Say the manoeuvre in plain language — “pincer with two squads, feint with the third” — and an LLM turns it into structured MCP calls that the OpenRA engine executes.

  • 用 17 个工具 + 2 个小队原语，实现钳形攻势、佯攻偷袭、路线约束、分时进攻等 10 类战术。
  • 设计取向是「人在回路」：玩家保留战略判断，AI 只做执行层的副驾驶，而不是替玩家打游戏。
  • 17 tools + 2 squad primitives express 10 tactics: pincer, feint-and-raid, route constraints, timed assaults.
  • Human-in-the-loop by design: the player keeps strategy; the AI is a co-pilot for execution, not a replacement player.

「你最像哪位中国皇帝？」人格测试“Which Chinese Emperor Are You?”

36 道历史情境题（另有 12 题简版），根据你的选择匹配一位中国皇帝，并附皇帝图鉴。

A 36-scenario quiz (plus a 12-question quick mode) that matches you to a Chinese emperor, with a collectible gallery.

每日完成测试次数（2026.04.13 – 04.28，数据来自自建埋点表）。4 月 20 日的断崖是服务器停服造成的。 Daily completed sessions, 13–28 Apr 2026 (from my own event table). The cliff on 20 Apr is the server being shut down.
  • 16 天累计完成 143,040 次，上线第 2 天单日 88,196 次。
  • 完整版占 63%，作答时长中位数 4 分钟，说明多数用户愿意认真做完。
  • 接入 Google Analytics，并自建逐题埋点（模式、时长、每题答案）分析结果分布。
  • 143,040 completed sessions in 16 days; 88,196 on day 2 alone.
  • 63% chose the full version; median session 4 minutes — most people actually finished it properly.
  • Google Analytics plus my own per-answer event table (mode, duration, each answer) to study result distribution.
复盘：服务器费用太高，我停了服，日完成量一天内从 3,745 掉到 189。这类测试本可以用静态托管加一个很轻的后端，成本几乎为零。 我学到的是：上线前就要算清楚流量成本，并想好流量来了以后怎么承接、怎么变现。
Retrospective: hosting costs got too high, I shut the server down, and daily completions dropped from 3,745 to 189 overnight. A static front end with a thin back end would have cost almost nothing. Lesson: model traffic cost — and how to capture and monetise a spike — before launch.

Obsidian Diary Bot · AI 日记陪伴机器人· AI journaling companion

2026.05 · 独立设计与开发 · 开源May 2026 · solo design & build · open source

在 Telegram 里随手写，AI 陪你聊几句、帮你反思，然后自动整理成 Obsidian 日记，附时间线、人物关系图和心情热力图。

Write in Telegram whenever you like; an AI companion reflects with you, then files it as an Obsidian daily note with a timeline, people graph and mood heatmap.

  • 按生活领域聊：工作、家庭、朋友、健康等 8 个话题，每个话题各自积累一份「你在这方面是什么样的人」的画像。
  • 不让 AI 千篇一律：回复策略按比例设计，60% 反思加追问，25% 纯共情，15% 指出你自己没注意到的规律。
  • 零成本运行：Cloudflare Worker + D1/KV + DeepSeek API，全在免费额度内；笔记写进你自己的 Obsidian 库。
  • Organised by life area: 8 topics (work, family, friends, health …), each building its own profile of who you are in that area.
  • Never formulaic: replies are deliberately mixed — 60% reflect-and-ask, 25% pure empathy, 15% pointing out patterns you hadn’t noticed.
  • Zero running cost: Cloudflare Worker + D1/KV + DeepSeek API on free tiers; notes land in your own vault.
