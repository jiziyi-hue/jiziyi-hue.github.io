测测你骨子里最像哪位中国皇帝Which Chinese emperor are you? 36 道情境题 · 50 位皇帝 · 结果图鉴，专为截图分享设计36 scenarios · 50 emperors · a collectible gallery built to be screenshotted and shared

每日完成测试次数（2026.04.13 – 04.28，自建埋点表）。灰色是 4 月 20 日停服之后。 Daily completed sessions, 13–28 Apr 2026, from my own event table. Grey bars: after the server shutdown on 20 Apr.
  • 完整版占 63%，作答时长中位数 4 分钟：多数人愿意认真做完。
  • 接入 Google Analytics，并自建逐题埋点（模式、时长、每题答案），用来分析结果分布和题目区分度。
  • 63% chose the full version; median session 4 minutes, so most people finished it properly.

复盘：服务器按流量计费太贵，停服后日完成量从 3,745 掉到 189；本可以用免费静态托管 + 轻量后端。 另外 35.2% 的结果都是汉武帝，分布过于集中，下一步会用逐题数据重新校准匹配。
Retrospective: traffic-billed hosting got too expensive; after shutdown, daily completions fell from 3,745 to 189. Free static hosting plus a thin back end would have done. Also, 35.2% of results were the same emperor; the next step is recalibrating the matching with the per-answer data.
